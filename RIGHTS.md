## Classification labels

Every animation's `meta.json` declares exactly one classification:

- `PHYSICALLY_MODELLED`
- `MATHEMATICALLY_INSPIRED`
- `SPECULATIVE_ARTISTIC`

Descriptions involving quantum theory, cosmology or high-dimensional physics are labelled
`MATHEMATICALLY_INSPIRED` or `SPECULATIVE_ARTISTIC` unless the underlying numerical method
genuinely models the claimed equation.
