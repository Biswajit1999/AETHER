ABOUT AETHER
A place for learning through motion.
AETHER is Biswajit Jana’s evolving computational visual laboratory: a personal space for studying mathematics, physics, sound, code and the structures that emerge between them.
Every work is presented as what it is—scientific model, mathematical construction, or visual experiment—with its assumptions made visible.
